# WARRNER LEGAL
## Digital Brand Guidelines & Website Style Guide

### Section 1: Brand Essence
The WARRNER LEGAL brand visualizes a fusion of classical legal authority with technical precision and global reach. It does not appear as a typical "stuffy" law firm; it appears as an analytical, global consultancy.

**Core Brand Pillars:**
1. **Precision:** Represented by the coordinates and the crosshair dot. We are exact.
2. **Global Perspective:** Represented by the wireframe globe. We see the big picture.
3. **Heritage & Trust:** Represented by the elegant serif typography. We understand the foundation of law.

### Section 3: Typography
The typography must reflect the two styles in the logo: the classical serif name and the functional sans-serif details.

**Font Principle:**
* **WARRNER (Serif):** Authority, tradition, trust.
* **LEGAL & Coordinates (Sans-Serif):** Modernity, efficiency, precision.

#### Recommended Web Fonts

| Hierarchy | Purpose | Font Family | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Headline** | Page Titles, Large Headers (H1, H2) | **Libre Baskerville** or **Playfair Display** | `Warrner Legal Services` |
| **Secondary Headline** | Section Headers, Subheaders (H3, H4) | **Montserrat** (Light/Regular) | `OUR PRACTICE AREAS` |
| **Body & UI Text** | Paragraphs, Buttons, Forms | **Montserrat** or **Open Sans** | `Warrner Legal is located at...` |

---

### Section 4: Imagery & Graphic Assets

#### A. The Logo As a Graphic System
The logo is not just a stamp; it is a source of design elements.

1. **The Globe Wireframe:** This can be extracted and used as a faint, watermark-style background pattern on section headers or the homepage "hero" section. *It must remain very light (Precision Taupe at 10-15% opacity).*
2. **The Crosshair Dot:** This specific maroon circle should be used as the design template for **Website UI Buttons** or **List Bullet Points**.

#### B. Photographic Style
Photography must reinforce "precision" and "perspective."

* **Allowed:** High-resolution architectural photography of modern legal spaces, close-ups of legal texts, aerial photography of cityscape grids (emphasizing coordinates), and professional, warm headshots of attorneys.
* **Avoid:** Generic, staged courthouse shots, handshake stocks, gavel images, or dark/moody photography.
